# Credits

Keep100 stands on other people's work. Every adapted skill names its sources at the top of its file too.

## Frameworks

The positioning, landing page and research skills are built on published frameworks from:

- **April Dunford**: *Obviously Awesome* and *Sales Pitch* (positioning components, competitive alternatives)
- **Anthony Pierri and Robert Kaminski, Fletch PMM**: minimum viable positioning, product-led versus audience-led messaging, homepage hero formula
- **Bob Moesta**: *Demand-Side Sales 101*, Jobs to Be Done interviews and the Four Forces
- **Joanna Wiebe, Copyhackers**: message mining and copy formulas
- **Katelyn Bourgoin, Customer Camp**: trigger events and the four buyer questions
- **Peep Laja, Wynter**: message clarity layers and message testing

## Skills

- **grill-me** by Matt Pocock inspired the Pressure test skill (interview relentlessly, one question at a time, recommend an answer each time). Keep100's version is rewritten to read the founder's company files.
- The always-on writing check draws on the widely shared catalogue of AI writing tells (puffery, rule-of-three padding, "-ing" filler, hedging). Keep100's version is written from scratch.

Something missing or wrong? Open an issue and we'll fix it.
