# Credits

Keep100 stands on other people's work. Every adapted skill names its sources at the top of its file too.

## Frameworks

The positioning, landing page and research skills are built on published frameworks from:

- **April Dunford**: *Obviously Awesome* and *Sales Pitch* (positioning components, competitive alternatives)
- **Anthony Pierri and Robert Kaminski, Fletch PMM**: minimum viable positioning, product-led versus audience-led messaging, homepage hero formula
- **Bob Moesta**: *Demand-Side Sales 101*, Jobs to Be Done interviews and the Four Forces
- **Joanna Wiebe, Copyhackers**: message mining and copy formulas
- **Katelyn Bourgoin, Customer Camp**: trigger events and the four buyer questions
- **Peep Laja, Wynter**: message clarity layers and message testing
- **Maja Voje**: *Go-To-Market Strategist* (choosing the first segment to win)
- **Emily Kramer, MKT1**: writing for the champion, the five-line persona
- **Lenny Rachitsky**: the checklist of defining customer traits
- **Matt Lerner**: finding the customer's words before writing copy
- **Rob Fitzpatrick**: *The Mom Test* (asking about what people did, not what they think)
- **Alex Hormozi**: *$100M Offers* (the value equation behind the offer skill)

## Skills

- **UI/UX Pro Max** by NextLevelBuilder (https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill, MIT, https://uupm.cc) is bundled in `skills/ui-ux-pro-max/` with its license, from commit 2de1134 (v2.15.0). Three things were changed: the script paths in its SKILL.md, its description (narrowed so it loads for the founder's own product pages), and the test folder (removed). Its scripts and data are untouched. Keep100's `landing-page-design` skill uses it to design the founder's landing page.

- **Marketing Skills** by Corey Haines (https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills, MIT). Six Keep100 skills are adapted from it at commit 7eb2a21, each rewritten for a solo founder with no customers: `customer-conversations` (from customer-research), `prospect-list` (from prospecting), `sales-replies` (from the cold-email reply reference and sales-enablement), `offer` (from offers), `launch` (from launch and directory-submissions) and `activation` (from signup and onboarding). `cold-email/SENDING.md` is adapted from its deliverability reference. The trigger eval idea in `evals/` comes from the same repo.

- **grill-me** by Matt Pocock inspired the Pressure test skill (interview relentlessly, one question at a time, recommend an answer each time). Keep100's version is rewritten to read the founder's company files.
- The writing check draws on the widely shared catalogue of AI writing tells (puffery, rule-of-three padding, "-ing" filler, hedging). Keep100's version is written from scratch.

Something missing or wrong? Open an issue and we'll fix it.
