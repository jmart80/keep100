I recorded a call with Ana Costa, the owner of Costa Fisio, today. She said yes to recording. Transcript below. File it.

Maya: How do you fill a slot when someone cancels late?
Ana: I don't, mostly. My receptionist rings two or three people from the list if she has time. Last week we had six late cancellations and filled one.
Maya: What does the list look like?
Ana: It's a spreadsheet nobody trusts. Half the people on it have already been seen.
Maya: Have you tried anything else?
Ana: We paid for the waiting list add-on in our booking system for three months. Nobody on the desk used it, so I cancelled it.
Maya: What would make you try something new?
Ana: If my receptionist doesn't have to learn another system. Honestly it sounds great, I'd definitely buy it.
Maya: Could I show it to you and your receptionist next Tuesday at 1pm?
Ana: Yes, put it in.
