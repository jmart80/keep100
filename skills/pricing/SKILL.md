# Pricing

The goal is a **first price that gets paying customers and teaches you something**, not the perfect price. It can change next month.

## Inputs

`company/positioning.md` (who, and what we beat), `company/competitors.md` and the pricing pattern in the latest research HANDOFF, `company/pricing.md` (anything already decided), `company/customers.md` (what they pay for the alternative today).

## Steps, one question at a time, recommending an answer each time

1. **What they pay today for the alternative.** A tool, a freelancer, staff hours, or lost money from doing nothing. This anchors value, not your costs.
2. **What the customer is buying.** The outcome, in their words. Price that, not features.
3. **Model.** Recommend the simplest that fits:
   - B2B: flat monthly per company, or per seat only if value grows with people. Annual option at about two months free.
   - B2C: monthly and annual subscription, or one-time purchase if it's used once.
   - Usage-based only if the customer naturally thinks in units (per report, per order).
4. **Free plan or free trial.** Default recommendation: a free trial (7 to 14 days) rather than a free plan, because a free plan attracts people who never pay and a solo founder can't support them. Free plan only if the product spreads by people inviting others.
5. **Number of plans.** Start with one paid plan, two at most. More plans means more decisions for buyers and more work for you.
6. **The price.** Place it against the alternatives from research. Early-stage founders almost always underprice; recommend erring higher and offering a founding-customer discount, rather than starting low and raising later.
7. **Founding-customer offer.** A clear, time-limited reason to buy now (for example, a locked-in price for the first 20 customers). Only offers recorded in `pricing.md` may ever be used in copy or emails.

## Write it down

Fill `company/pricing.md`: plans, prices, trial, why these prices (one line each, with the research behind them), discounts allowed. Confirm with the founder, set `Status: Confirmed`, add the prices to `facts.md` as `[confirmed]`. Log in `progress.md` and propose the landing page next.

Note when to revisit: after the first 10 paying customers, or if nobody objects to the price at all (a sign it's too low).
