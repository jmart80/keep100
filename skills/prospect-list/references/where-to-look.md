# Where to look

Use the customer's own words from `company/customers.md` in every search. People describe their problem in their vocabulary, not the product's.

## Trigger lane

A trigger is something that happened, with a date, that makes the problem more urgent for this company now.

| Trigger | Where it shows up | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring for the role that owns the problem | Company careers page, public job boards | They have budget and a gap |
| A new person in the champion's role | Company news, the person's public profile | New people change tools in their first months |
| A launch, expansion or new location | Company blog, press, launch sites | More volume breaks the old way |
| A change to a tool or rule they depend on | Vendor announcements, regulator notices, trade press | Forced change |
| The champion wrote or spoke about the problem | Their posts, a podcast, a conference talk | They are already thinking about it |
| A competitor's customer is unhappy in public | Reviews, community threads | Already looking |

Search pattern: the company type plus the trigger plus a time limit. Then open each company's own site to confirm the event and find the person who owns the problem.

## Pain signal lane

| Signal | Phrases to search, in the customer's words | Read as |
|---|---|---|
| Asking for a tool | "looking for", "recommend", "alternative to", "does anything exist that", "how do you all handle" | Highest intent |
| Describing the pain | "takes hours", "so manual", "keeps breaking", "biggest frustration", "why is there no" | Aware of the problem |
| Showing a workaround | spreadsheets, copy and paste, a hired helper, a chain of small tools, a script | Already paying in time |
| Leaving something | "cancelled", "moving off", "switched from", "too expensive for what it does" | In motion |

Places: the subreddits, forums, groups and communities listed under "Where they spend time" in `company/customers.md`. Review sites for the competitors in `company/competitors.md`. Public issue trackers and launch sites when the customer is technical. App store reviews when the customer is a consumer.

## What counts as evidence

- A link to the page, the date it was posted, and one line of what was said or what happened
- Posted by this person or about this company. A general article about the industry is context, not evidence
- Recent. Older than 60 days lowers the grade

## What to skip

- Private groups and gated communities
- Anyone venting about a personal hardship
- Pages that need a login you would have to work around
- Lists that summarize other people's posts. Find the original
