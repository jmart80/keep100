# Writing check

Run this silently on every draft meant for the outside world. Show the founder the fixed version, plus a one-line note only if you removed a claim they need to know about.

## 1. Facts

- Read `company/facts.md`. Every factual claim about the product, customers, results, numbers or the company must match a `[confirmed]` fact.
- Unconfirmed or missing claim: cut it, or replace it with something confirmed. Tell the founder in one line which claim you cut and why, so they can confirm it if it's true.
- Never allowed unless confirmed: numbers, user counts, customer names, testimonials, ratings, logos, "trusted by", results.

## 2. Brand

- Read `company/brand.md`. Use their words, avoid their banned words, match their voice and the exact spelling of their name.

## 3. AI tells

Fix every one of these:

**Words:** delve, leverage, utilize, streamline, seamless, robust, empower, unlock, elevate, supercharge, revolutionize, transform, game-changer, cutting-edge, innovative, world-class, best-in-class, holistic, synergy, journey, landscape, realm, tapestry, testament, pivotal, crucial, comprehensive, foster, navigate (figuratively), embark, craft, harness.

**Openers and closers:** "In today's fast-paced world", "I hope this finds you well", "I wanted to reach out", "Let's dive in", "In conclusion", "Ready to take your X to the next level?", "Whether you're X or Y".

**Patterns:**
- Puffery: "a pivotal moment", "setting the stage". State what happened.
- Trailing "-ing" filler: "..., ensuring smooth delivery", "..., highlighting the value". Cut or make it its own concrete sentence.
- Rule of three by reflex: "fast, simple, and powerful". Keep one, make it specific.
- "Not just X, but Y" and "It's not about X, it's about Y".
- Hedge stacking: "can potentially help". Say what it does.
- Em dashes as separators. Use a full stop or a comma.
- Every sentence the same length. Vary it.
- Vague praise: "amazing results". Name the result or cut it.

## 4. Read it as a stranger

Would the reader know what this is about from the first line? If not, rewrite the first line.

## 5. Sound like a person

Removing tells isn't enough. Prefer short concrete sentences, the customer's own words, and one opinion over a balanced list. Contractions are fine.
