# Research rules

- Every claim gets a source link. Label your own conclusions as inference.
- A quote is `[verbatim]` only when you saw the exact words in the page text. A search summary or your memory of a page is a `[paraphrase]`. Never put a paraphrase inside quotation marks.
- Say whose customer spoke: ours, a prospect, or a competitor's customer.
- Date every competitor fact. Prices and features change.
- When research changes something in `company/` (a new competitor, a pricing pattern), propose the change to the founder. Don't edit company files on your own.
