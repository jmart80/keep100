# Social rules

- Posts are built from customer language in `company/customers.md` and research quotes, not from generic marketing angles.
- One idea per post. Hook in the first line.
- Match the platform: what works on TikTok fails on LinkedIn.
- Read `learnings.md` before writing, so each week builds on what worked.
- Log every post published in `posted.csv`, with its link.
- In communities, help first and follow each community's rules.
