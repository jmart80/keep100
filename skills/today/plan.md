# The 30-day plan

Days are a guide. Order matters more than dates.

## Both tracks

| Day | Work | Skill | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setup, brand, homepage headline | setup | ~1 hour |
| 2 | Competitor research | competitor-research | ~30 min |
| 3 | Positioning: who it's for, why you win, what to say | positioning | ~1 hour |
| 4 | Pricing | pricing | ~30 min |
| 5 | Landing page copy | landing-page | ~1 hour |
| 6 | Welcome and activation emails (if they have signups or a trial) | product-emails | ~30 min |

## B2B track: days 6 to 30

| Days | Work | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Build a list of 30 to 50 ideal prospects: named people at named companies, each with a reason to write now | cold-email |
| 7 | Write the 5-email sequence and send the first 10 | cold-email |
| 8 to 30, daily | Send 10 new first emails, send the follow-ups that are due, reply to every response within a day, log everything | cold-email |
| Weekly | Look at replies: which subject line and angle got answers? Adjust the sequence | cold-email |
| Any reply | Help prepare for the call: questions to ask, what to offer, how to ask for the sale | pressure-test |
| 10, 20 | Optional: one LinkedIn post about the problem, in customer language | social-content |
| 24 to 30 | Start content and SEO: 2 posts on the questions prospects asked | content-seo |

## B2C track: days 6 to 30

| Days | Work | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Pick 1 or 2 platforms where the ideal customer actually spends time, and 3 content pillars from their struggles | social-content |
| 7 | Write the first week of posts and short-video scripts | social-content |
| 8 to 30, daily | Publish 1 post or video, reply to every comment, join 3 conversations where customers already talk (subreddits, groups), log everything | social-content |
| Weekly | Look at which posts got saves, shares or signups. Do more of that, drop the rest | social-content |
| 10 onward | Upgrade email for free users or trial users | product-emails |
| 24 to 30 | Start content and SEO: 2 posts answering the questions customers ask | content-seo |

## Anytime

- New idea or big decision: `pressure-test`.
- Anything published or sent: `writing-check` first.
