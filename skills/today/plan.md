# The plan

Steps are worked in order, top to bottom of `progress.md`. The day numbers are the pace for a founder with two hours a day. Order matters more than dates. With one hour a day, the same steps take about twice as long.

## Setup and foundation, both tracks

| Step in progress.md | Pace | Skill | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup (five steps) | Day 1 | setup | ~1 hour |
| Competitor research | Day 2 | competitor-research | ~30 min |
| Customer conversations invited | Day 2 | customer-conversations | ~20 min |
| Positioning | Day 3 | positioning | ~1 hour |
| Pricing and a way to pay | Day 4 | pricing | ~30 min |
| Founding offer | Day 4 | offer | ~30 min |
| Landing page copy | Day 5 | landing-page | ~1 hour |
| Landing page live | Day 6 | landing-page-design | ~1 to 2 hours |
| Signup path checked | Day 7 | activation | ~45 min |
| Product emails | Day 7 | product-emails | ~30 min |

Skip "Signup path checked" and "Product emails" with a reason if nobody can sign up yet. If the product is not live, the founding offer step uses the pre-sale branch of `offer`.

## Getting customers, B2B

| Step in progress.md | Pace | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| Sending setup | Day 6 | cold-email (`SENDING.md`) |
| First prospect list | Day 7 | prospect-list |
| Email sequence written | Day 8 | cold-email |
| First emails sent | Day 8 | cold-email |
| First reply | When it comes | sales-replies |
| First sales call | When it comes | sales-replies |
| Launch in three places | From day 10, one place a week | launch |

**Daily rhythm, from the first send:** today's queue from `prospect-list` (follow-ups due, then new first emails up to `Daily new emails:`), drafts from `cold-email`, every reply answered the same day with `sales-replies`.

## Getting customers, B2C

| Step in progress.md | Pace | Skill |

| First post published | Day 8 | social-content |

## Weekly rhythm, both tracks, from the first week of selling

| When | Work | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| Any day | One or two customer conversations, processed the same day | customer-conversations |
| Monday | Weekly brief | weekly-brief |
| Friday | Review: the week's numbers into Weekly numbers in `progress.md`, two or three lines into `learnings.md`, the list rebuilt (B2B) | prospect-list, today |
| Monthly | Workspace audit | workspace-audit |

After every step is ticked, this rhythm is the plan: conversations, the daily customer work, one experiment a week, and the Friday review.

## Anytime

- New idea or big decision: `pressure-test`.
- Anything published or sent: `writing-check` first.
- "Show me where I am": `dashboard`.
