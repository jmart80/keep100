# Content and SEO

Search and AI-answer traffic takes months to build, so this starts around day 24, after customer-getting work is running. If the founder asks earlier, help, but say plainly that outreach or social will bring customers faster this month.

## Inputs

`company/customers.md` (the questions and words customers use), `company/positioning.md`, `company/competitors.md`, research, and the questions prospects asked in the Getting customers log.

## Branch A: Topics and keywords

1. Start from **real questions** customers ask: from replies, comments, calls, reviews and communities. These beat keyword tools for a new site.
2. With web search or Exa, check each: who ranks now, how strong those pages are, and whether the searcher wants to buy, compare or learn.
3. Prioritize, in this order: comparison and alternative pages ("{competitor} alternative", "{competitor} vs {product}"), problem-specific how-tos the product genuinely helps with, then broader education.
4. Save a ranked list of 10 to 20 topics to `marketing/content-plan.md`, each with the target phrase, the question it answers, and why it should win.

## Branch B: Write a post

- Answer the question in the first two sentences. Then the detail.
- Use the customer's words for the question and headings.
- Be specific: real steps, real examples, the founder's own experience. Generic posts don't rank and don't get cited.
- Mention the product only where it truly helps, once or twice, never forced.
- Only confirmed facts. Cite sources for outside claims with links.
- Include a title (under 60 characters), a meta description (under 155 characters), and a URL slug.
- Run `keep100:writing-check`, save to `marketing/<date>-<slug>.md`.

## Branch C: Show up in AI answers (GEO)

AI assistants cite pages that state facts clearly and are referenced elsewhere. So:
- A clear, factual "what is {product}" page and a pricing page in plain HTML text, not only images.
- Comparison pages that are honest about where competitors are better.
- An FAQ answering real questions in one or two sentences each.
- Getting mentioned where AI tools look: relevant Reddit threads, directories in the niche, roundup posts, and genuine answers in communities.
- Check now and then: ask a few AI assistants the questions customers ask, and note whether and how the product appears.
