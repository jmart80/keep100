# Banned Words, Phrases, and Tells

Zero tolerance. One hit fails the pre-send gate.

## Overused AI/corporate jargon

streamline, leverage, elevate, optimize, seamless, robust, scalable, innovative, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, transformative, world-class, best-in-class, empower, unlock, delve, dive into, embark on, synergy, utilize, facilitate, comprehensive, pivotal, crucial, vital, paradigm shift, game-changer, solution-oriented, holistic, proactive, forward-thinking, actionable, impactful, resonates, compelling, craft, crafted

## AI transitional tells

Furthermore, moreover, consequently, thus, accordingly, nonetheless, subsequently

## Banned opening phrases

- "Hope this finds you well"
- "I wanted to reach out"
- "I'm excited to share"
- "I'm following up on my previous email"
- "Just checking in"
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "It's worth noting that"
- "As previously mentioned"

## Structural tells

- Em dashes used as stylistic separators
- Semicolons in conversational sentences
- Numbered or bulleted lists inside the email body (single exception: the email 5 breakup multiple-choice list)
