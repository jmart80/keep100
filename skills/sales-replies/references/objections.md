# Early objections

An objection in the first reply is usually a reflex. Answer once. Every answer below ends by making no an easy option, because the people who feel free to say no are the ones who say yes later.

Fill each answer from the company files. If the file has nothing that fits, leave the proof out. Never invent a customer, a number or a comparison.

| They say | What it usually means | Shape of the answer |
|---|---|---|
| "We already use {tool}" | They have a way that works well enough | Agree it does the job for most people. Name the one situation where people like them still look elsewhere, from `company/positioning.md`. Ask if that situation is theirs |
| "No budget" | Not a priority, or the wrong person | Ask when they next plan spending. Or offer the no-cost thing agreed with the founder |
| "Not a priority right now" | True | Ask what is, this quarter. If the problem is unrelated, thank them and set a date to check back |
| "Send me more information" | A polite exit, or real interest with no time | Send one thing, such as a short page or a two-minute video. Ask one question that is easy to answer |
| "How much is it?" | Interest | Give the price from `company/pricing.md` plainly. Then one line on what it replaces |
| "That's too expensive" | Compared to what they pay now, or to nothing | Ask what they are comparing it to. Restate what the alternative costs them today, from their own words if they gave any. Never discount beyond `company/pricing.md` |
| "Is it secure? Where is my data?" | A real blocker | Answer only from confirmed facts. If the file is silent, say you will find out, and ask the founder |
| "You're very new" | Fear of being stranded | Agree. Offer what a new company can: direct access to the founder, fast fixes, and the guarantee in `company/pricing.md` if one is recorded |
| "I need to ask my {boss, partner}" | They are not the only decision maker | Offer to make it easy: a three-line summary they can forward, or a short call with both |
| "How did you get my email?" | Wary | Tell them the true source from `email_source` in the pipeline file. Offer to remove them |
| "Can it do {feature}?" | Testing fit | If `company/product.md` says yes, say yes and how. If it says no, say no, and ask what they would use it for |

## Tone

- Agree with what is true in the objection first
- One or two sentences, then a question or an exit
- No pressure, no countdowns, nothing that is not in the files
