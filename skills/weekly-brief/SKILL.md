# Weekly brief

A short Monday message. It is about **next actions, not metrics**. In the first month numbers are small, and a scorecard full of zeros demotivates. Never lead with or chart metrics; mention a paying customer only to celebrate it.

## Steps

1. Read `progress.md` and the `Status:` line of every file in `company/`.
2. Write the brief, under 200 words:

```
# Week of {date} · Day {n} of 30

## Where you are
{One or two sentences. What's done, what's next in the plan.}
{If setup or a foundation step is unfinished: name it as the first thing to do this week, with how long it takes.}

## Three things to draft this week
1. {Specific idea, e.g. "A LinkedIn post about {verbatim struggle from customers.md}"}
2. {e.g. "A blog post answering '{question a prospect asked}'"}
3. {e.g. "Follow-ups to the 6 people who opened but didn't reply"}

Reply with a number and I'll draft it.
```

3. Ideas must be specific to their company: built from customer quotes, competitor gaps in `research/`, questions in the Getting customers log, or their track's playbook. Never generic advice like "post consistently".
4. Before day 24, ideas favour their track's customer-getting work. From day 24, include at least one content or SEO idea.
5. Save to `briefs/{date}.md`.
