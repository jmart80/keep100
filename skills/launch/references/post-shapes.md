# Post shapes

Open each place's own rules before using a shape. If the rules forbid self-promotion, do not post a launch there. Answer questions instead and let the profile link do the work.

## Community post

For forums, subreddits and groups where the customer already talks.

```
Title: {The problem or the finding, in the customer's words. Not the product name.}

{Two or three sentences on the problem as the founder saw it, with one specific detail.}

{What the founder learned or built in response. Something useful even to a reader who never clicks.}

{One or two sentences: "I built {product} to do this. It's {what is live today}."}

{A real question for the reader, the kind the founder wants answered.}

{Link, once. "I'm the founder" stated plainly.}
```

- No formatting tricks, no emoji rows
- Answer every comment the same day, including the critical ones
- If someone asks a question the product does not solve, say so

## Launch site post

For sites built for announcing products.

```
Tagline: {One line from the listing kit}

{The medium description.}

{Founder's note.}

{What is live, what is coming, and the founding offer if open.}

{An invitation to ask anything.}
```

- Be present for the whole first day
- Tell the people who already know the product that it is live. Do not ask anyone for votes

## Technical audience post

For places where builders read. Lead with how it works and what was hard.

```
Title: Show: {product}. {What it does, plainly}

{What it is in one sentence.}
{Why the existing ways fall short, specifically.}
{How it works, with one interesting technical or design decision.}
{What it does not do yet.}
{Link.}
```

- Plain claims only. This audience checks them
- Welcome criticism and answer it with specifics

## Pitch to a newsletter, podcast or creator

Under 80 words.

```
Subject: {something for their readers, in a few words}

Hi {first_name},

{One sentence showing the founder reads or watches them, with a specific recent piece.}

{One sentence on what the founder built and why their audience has this problem.}

{The offer for their audience: early access, the founding price, or a finding from the founder's research.}

{One easy question: "Worth a look?"}

{Founder}
```

- One follow-up after a week, then stop
- If anything of value changes hands, the resulting post must say so
