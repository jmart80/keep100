# Invitations

Under 60 words. From the founder, in the first person. No link to the product, no description of it beyond a few words. The reason for choosing this person is specific and true.

## To a user or customer

```
Subject: 20 minutes about how you use {product}

Hi {first_name},

You've {specific thing they did, e.g. "run 14 reports this month"}, which is more than almost anyone. I'd like to hear how it fits into your week and what annoys you about it. 20 minutes, no pitch.

Does {day} or {day} work?

{Founder}
```

## To someone who replied to outreach or joined a waitlist

```
Subject: quick question about {the problem, in their words}

Hi {first_name},

Thanks for {replying | signing up}. Before I build more, I want to understand how you handle {problem} today. Could I ask you about it for 20 minutes? I'll share what I learn across everyone I talk to.

{Day} or {day}?

{Founder}
```

## To someone who posted publicly about the problem

Reply where they posted, if replies are normal there. Do not move to a private message unless they invite it.

```
I'm researching exactly this. What have you tried so far? I'm talking to {role}s this month and happy to share what I find. If you're open to a 20-minute call, I'd value it.
```

## Checks before sending

- The reason for choosing them is something only true of them
- One ask, two times
- No product pitch and no link
- It reads like a person wrote it to one person
