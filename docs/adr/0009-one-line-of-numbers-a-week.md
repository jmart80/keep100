# 9. The weekly brief carries one line of numbers

Date: 2026-11-20
Status: Accepted. Amends the Weekly brief entry in the glossary.

## Context

The Weekly brief was defined as next actions with no metrics, because a scorecard of zeros in month one demotivates. That left the Founder with no way to tell a list problem from a copy problem from a price problem. "Nobody is replying" and "people reply but nobody buys" need different work, and only numbers tell them apart.

## Decision

The brief and the dashboard show one funnel line: reached, replies or signups, calls, paid. On B2B it is computed from the Pipeline. On B2C the Founder counts it by hand on Fridays. There are no charts, no trends and no targets. The line exists to point at the stage losing people, and the daily driver routes to the skill for that stage.

The Kit states no benchmark rates. Thresholds for when to stop and rethink are left to the Founder's own counts until sourced research supports a number.

## Consequences

- A Founder sees zeros early. The brief pairs the line with what to do about it.
- With fewer than about thirty sends, the script says the rate means little and to look at who replied.
- No skill may quote a typical conversion or reply rate from memory.
