# Customers

Status: Confirmed
Last confirmed: 2026-11-16

## Who pays
People (B2C).

## Ideal customer
Adults in their thirties and forties who have signed up for their first half marathon and can run 5 km without stopping.

## What they struggle with, in their own words
- [competitor's customer] [paraphrase] The plan wanted five runs a week and they quit by week three. (source: https://reviews.example/big-run-app/771)
- [prospect] [paraphrase] Missing one week felt like the whole plan was ruined. (source: https://forum.example/first-half/t/902)

## What they use today instead
A free PDF plan, a big running app's plan, or a friend's advice.

## Where they spend time
- The First Half forum (forum.example/first-half)
- Short running videos on Instagram and TikTok

## What makes them hesitate
- "Will I actually use it?"
- Paying before knowing if the plan fits their week.
