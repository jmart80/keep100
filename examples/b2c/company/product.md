# Product

Status: Confirmed
Last confirmed: 2026-11-16

## In one sentence
Slowmile is a training plan app for adults running their first half marathon, built around three runs a week.

## What it does
- Builds a 16-week plan from your current longest run and your race date
- Three runs a week, never more
- Moves the plan when you miss a week
- Works on iPhone and Android

## What it does not do
- No GPS tracking. It reads runs from your watch app or you tick them off
- No coaching by a person
- No plans for other distances yet

## How someone starts using it
Free download. The first four weeks of a plan are free.
