- Outreach opens a conversation. It never pitches hard or pressures.
- Personalize with a real reason to write to this person. Never fake familiarity.
- Only send-ready drafts here. The founder sends from their own email. Claude never sends on their behalf.
- `pipeline.csv` is the only record of who was contacted. Update it the same day. Don't copy contacts into `progress.md`.
- Anyone who asks to stop is marked `stopped` and never written to again.
- Never guess an email address.
