# Positioning

Status: Provisional
Last confirmed: 2026-11-06

## Alternatives we beat
A paper waiting list and phone calls. The waiting list feature inside a full practice suite. Leaving the slot empty.

## What only we have
Texting the waiting list automatically the moment a slot opens, and booking the first patient who accepts.

## Why that matters to the customer
Cancelled slots get filled without the front desk making calls. Unproven in numbers so far.

## Category
Appointment scheduling for physiotherapy clinics.

## Category awareness
Known.

## Who to win first
Owner-run physiotherapy clinics with 2 to 5 therapists that are hiring front desk staff or opening a second location.

## Champion
The clinic owner, who also treats patients and feels every empty slot.

## Trigger events
- Hiring for a front desk or reception role
- Opening a second location
- A public complaint about cancellations or no-shows

## Job to be done
Fill cancelled appointments without anyone ringing round.

## Top objection
"We already pay for a practice system."

## Value props
1. Late cancellation leaves an empty slot. Kneecap texts the waiting list at once. The slot is offered to patients before the front desk has picked up the phone.
2. The waiting list lives in a notebook. Kneecap keeps one per therapist. Nobody is forgotten.
3. Front desk time goes on calls. Patients book themselves from the text. The desk gets that time back.

## Headline candidates
- Fill cancelled physio appointments without ringing round ("I was the one ringing round between patients")
- Your waiting list, texted the moment a slot opens ("The waiting list is a page in a notebook")

## One-line positioning statement
Kneecap is appointment scheduling for small physiotherapy clinics that fills cancelled slots by texting the waiting list automatically.

## What to learn from the first customers
- How many cancelled slots get filled in a normal week
- Whether clinics run it beside their practice system or instead of its calendar
