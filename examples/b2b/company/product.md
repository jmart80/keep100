# Product

Status: Confirmed
Last confirmed: 2026-11-03

## In one sentence
Kneecap is appointment scheduling for independent physiotherapy clinics that fills cancelled slots from a waiting list by text message.

## What it does
- Online booking page for patients
- A waiting list per therapist
- When a patient cancels, Kneecap texts the next people on the waiting list and books the first to accept
- Appointment reminders by text
- A day view for the front desk

## What it does not do
- No billing, invoicing or insurance claims
- No clinical notes
- No integration with other practice software yet
- No mobile app. It runs in a browser

## How someone starts using it
A 14-day free trial, started from the website. No card asked for.

## Signup or payment link
https://kneecap.example/start

## Key action
Adding the first three patients to a waiting list.

## How the product sends email
By hand, from Maya's mailbox. The product sends only text reminders.

## Where it is today
Live since October 2026. Two clinics on trial. No paying customers yet.
