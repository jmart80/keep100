# Customers

Status: Confirmed
Last confirmed: 2026-11-06

## Who pays
Businesses (B2B).

## Ideal customer
Owner-run physiotherapy clinics with 2 to 5 therapists and one person on the front desk, who lose appointments to late cancellations.

## What they struggle with, in their own words
- [prospect] [verbatim] "I had four cancellations on Tuesday and filled one, because I was the one ringing round between patients." (source: conversations/2026-11-05-dan-clinic-owner.md)
- [prospect] [verbatim] "The waiting list is a page in a notebook at the front desk." (source: conversations/2026-11-05-dan-clinic-owner.md)
- [competitor's customer] [paraphrase] The big practice systems do everything, and the waiting list feature is buried and nobody on the desk uses it. (source: https://reviews.example/practice-suite/1842)
- [competitor's customer] [paraphrase] Paying for a full practice suite when all they wanted was fewer empty slots. (source: https://forum.example/physio-owners/t/3391)

## What they use today instead
A full practice management suite, a paper waiting list, phone calls from the front desk, or nothing.

## Where they spend time
- The Physio Owners forum (forum.example/physio-owners)
- A regional clinic owners group on Facebook
- The Clinic Business newsletter

## What makes them hesitate
- "We already pay for a practice system."
- Whether patients' phone numbers are safe.
- Front desk staff having to learn another tool.
